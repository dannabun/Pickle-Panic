Pickle Panic (changes from one-pager)

The main idea of Pickle Panic stayed the same: the player controls a paddle, returns incoming pickleballs, earns points, and tries to survive with three lives. I made several changes while implementing the game.

1. The court was changed from a traditional top-down view into a vertical player-perspective view. The paddle remains at the bottom while balls enter from the top.

2. I did not include a visible NPC opponent. Instead, balls are automatically spawned from random horizontal positions to keep the game focused on quick reaction-based gameplay.

3. The player character was simplified into a paddle so the game could focus more on timing, movement, and returning balls.

4. The game became more arcade-style and less realistic. The balls move vertically toward the player instead of following a full pickleball rally across the court.

5. Fireballs move faster and give bonus points. Bomb balls remove one life if the player hits them, but they are harmless if the player allows them to pass.

6. A level system was added. Ball speed gradually increases as the player’s score increases. A level-up sound plays every 1,000 points.

7. Sound effects were added for normal pickleball hits, fireball hits, level-ups, losing a life, and game over. 

8. The original simple ball shapes were replaced with visual images for the regular pickleball, fireball, and bomb.
